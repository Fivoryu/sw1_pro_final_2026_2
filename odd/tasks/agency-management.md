### Agreed product behavior

- Multi-tenant SaaS: `platform_admin` creates agencies and their administrators; `agency_admin` administers agency content and approves/rejects agent-prepared drafts; agents prepare properties with photos, rooms, furniture, prices, and editable simple geometry; clients browse approved offers and reserve with an external wallet.
- Tenant-private administrative data/operations must be isolated across agencies. Approved catalog publications are visible to clients across agencies.
- First delivery spatial scope is one-floor properties with manually connected rooms and simple editable primitives; no photorealistic reconstruction or automatic multi-floor stitching.
- Price base excludes optional furniture. For sale, optional items have one-time prices; for rent, base and optional-item prices are monthly. The server calculates and returns the breakdown; client-sent totals are not authoritative. Hiding furniture in the 3D view does not remove it from the offer or change price.
- Reservation is a pending commercial reservation with a fixed per-property test-token deposit, not a full payment. Only one pending reservation may exist per property. Pending cancellation or agency rejection calls for refund; no agency response in 24 hours expires the reservation. Acceptance reserves the property and preserves a commercial snapshot; legal closing happens outside RoomForge. The application does not custody private keys and does not automatically cancel a reservation after acceptance.

The product document does not define API paths/DTOs; the user has now approved this bounded implementation contract:

- `POST /api/v1/agencies` accepts an agency ID (`{"id":"<tenant-id>"}`); `GET /api/v1/agencies` lists agency IDs. Both require an active server-side `platform_admin` session.
- `POST /api/v1/agencies/{agency_id}/admin-invitations` accepts an email and creates a pending `agency_admin` invitation scoped to that agency. The invitation is delivered through the existing notifier; delivery failure revokes it, marks delivery failed, and never exposes the token.
- The invitee continues through `POST /api/v1/auth/invitations/accept` and the existing TOTP enrollment flow. The account role and tenant are taken from the stored invitation; client-provided role/tenant cannot override them.
- Agreed error semantics: 401 invalid/missing active session, 403 wrong role, 404 unknown agency, 409 duplicate agency ID or normalized invite email that already has any account, any unexpired pending invitation, or an accepted invitation with an active/unexpired TOTP enrollment (regardless of role/agency), and 422 invalid input. The user explicitly chose to reserve the email through TOTP completion or enrollment expiry; after successful enrollment the account blocks new invitations, and after challenge expiry the email may be invited again. Enforce conflicts atomically under concurrent requests, not only with read-before-insert checks.
- Verified success/error JSON: agency POST `201 {"id":"<tenant-id>"}`; agency list `200 {"agencies":[{"id":"<tenant-id>"}]}`; invitation POST `201 {"status":"sent"}`; email conflict `409 {"detail":"An account or active invitation already uses this email"}`; email-delivery failure `502 {"detail":"Invitation email delivery failed"}`. Delivery failure leaves the invitation revoked and `delivery_status="failed"`, and no raw token is returned. No property/reservation state names are introduced here.
- Concurrency enforcement: partial unique index `uq_staff_invitation_pending_normalized_email` on `lower(trim(email))` for `status='pending'`, across roles/agencies. Service translates only that named constraint violation to the approved 409. New migration `0004_staff_invitation_pending_email_unique` aborts without altering data if existing duplicate pending normalized-email groups are found. A shared PostgreSQL transaction advisory lock keyed deterministically by normalized email serializes agency invite issuance, platform-admin bootstrap issuance, invitation acceptance/challenge creation, and TOTP completion/account creation. The invitation service and bootstrap check existing accounts and active TOTP claims. TOTP lockout (`consumed_at` after five invalid codes) must continue reserving the email until challenge expiry; normalize a legacy invitation email before persisting its new StaffAccount.
